# HR context for product videos

Read this before writing a brief or a script. It sets the audience, the tone and the risks that are specific to HR.

## Who watches

| Audience | What they want from the video | Typical channel |
|---|---|---|
| All employees | What it is, how to start, why it helps them today | Email, Teams or Slack post, intranet, town hall |
| New joiners | Where to find things in their first weeks | Onboarding portal, induction session |
| People managers | How it helps them answer their team's questions | Manager briefings, leadership calls |
| HR teams (business partners, operations, COEs) | What changes in their work, what employees will ask | HR team meetings, demo sessions |
| Leadership | Why it matters, adoption, what comes next | Town hall, leadership review |
| Clients or prospects (only with approval) | What was built, outcomes | Client meetings, case studies |

Most HR product videos are for employees. Write for a busy person watching on a phone with the sound off, and for a
town hall screen with sound on. Captions serve both.

## HR areas and typical product videos

| Area | Example products and features | Useful video angle |
|---|---|---|
| Talent acquisition | Careers site, referral app, interview scheduling, candidate assistant | Fewer steps, faster updates |
| Onboarding | Joining portal, pre-joining tasks, induction assistant | Day one ready, one place for everything |
| HR policies | Policy assistant or chatbot, policy portal | Ask in plain language, answers with the source |
| HR service delivery | Service desk, ticketing, query routing | Raise once, track it, get a reply |
| Leave and attendance | Leave app, attendance, holiday calendar | Apply and check balance in seconds |
| Pay and benefits | Payslips, tax declarations, reimbursements, benefits enrolment | Clear, self-serve, on time |
| Learning | Learning platform, academies, skill paths | Find the right course, track progress |
| Performance | Goals, check-ins, feedback, reviews | Simple cycle, fewer forms |
| Careers and mobility | Internal jobs, gigs, stints, mentoring | Grow without leaving |
| Recognition | Rewards and recognition platform | Thank people in the moment |
| Wellbeing | Wellbeing app, assistance programme, health benefits | Support is easy and private |
| Diversity and inclusion | Programmes, networks, accessibility | Belonging, with real actions |
| People analytics | Dashboards for managers and HR | Better decisions, explained simply |
| Separation and exit | Exit process, clearance, alumni | Respectful, clear, quick |

## Tone

- Professional, warm and plain. Short sentences. Active voice. Speak to "you".
- Excited about the product, never hyped. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "unlock".
- Sentence case in all on-screen text. No all caps. No em dashes.
- Use the names employees use (the product name, the app name), not internal project names.
- Invite participation where the product is new: feedback shapes the product.
- Inclusive language. Avoid gendered defaults unless quoting a real policy.

## Sensitive topics: handle with care

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pay, grades, bonuses | Do not show grade-wise amounts or pay data in a video, especially for clients |
| Health, leave for medical reasons, wellbeing | General only. No case examples that could identify anyone |
| Grievances, harassment, disciplinary matters | Name the channel, never dramatise a case |
| Separation, layoffs, restructuring | Neutral and factual. Never light or playful |
| Legal and statutory claims | Only if the policy owner and legal confirm the wording |
| Regional differences | Make one version per region when policies differ. Never show one region's policy to another |

## Privacy

- Use fictional employee names and locations in rebuilt screens. Avoid a real colleague's name.
- Remove names, photos, chats, contacts, ticket numbers and notification badges from any screenshot.
- Real product answers are fine once the content owner confirms them; strip any personal data.

## Accuracy

- Show only features that are live on the release date. If the video plays before launch, avoid "now live".
- Every number and policy fact on screen must come from the product or the policy, and be confirmed by its owner.
- An answer that says it is guessing, contradicts itself or is outdated must not appear.

## Approvals to plan for

| Who | What they check |
|---|---|
| Content or policy owner | Every answer and fact shown |
| Internal communications | Message, channel, timing |
| Brand team | Logo use, colours, intro and outro changes |
| Legal (if external or statutory) | Claims, disclaimers |
| Information security (if screens are shown) | No sensitive data visible |

## Lengths that work

| Use | Length |
|---|---|
| Social or chat teaser | 15 to 30 seconds |
| Launch explainer | 60 to 90 seconds |
| Town hall feature | 90 to 120 seconds |
| How-to walkthrough | 2 to 4 minutes, in chapters |
